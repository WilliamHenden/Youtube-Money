# Video 2: America's Debt

**Status: IDEA STAGE. Groundwork only (13 November 2026). The owner has put this topic in the queue but has not yet picked a hook. No script exists.**

- **Working title:** Who Does America Owe $40 Trillion To?
- **Central question:** America owes more than forty trillion dollars. Who is it actually owed to, and why hasn't the country gone bust?
- **Surprising answer:** Mostly to Americans themselves. Only about a quarter is owed abroad, and China is not even the biggest foreign lender. America can keep borrowing because it borrows in a currency it creates and the world still wants its bonds. The real cost is the interest, which now tops a trillion dollars a year, more than the military.
- **Why a viewer cares:** everyone has heard the number and almost nobody knows who the lender is. It also shows the difference between owing money and being broke, which applies to households and companies too.
- **"Already knew that" test:** most viewers would guess "China". That guess being wrong is the hook.
- **Avoid:** the word "thrives" (owner's note), and any prediction of collapse. The honest position is "it works until the interest bill bites".

## Title options

1. Who Does America Owe $40 Trillion To?
2. America Owes $40 Trillion. Most of It Isn't to China.
3. Why America Hasn't Gone Bust (And What It Costs)

Use the exact current figure on publish day.

## Five hook options

1. **It isn't China (recommended).** Ask most people who America owes its money to and they'll say China. China holds a small slice. The biggest lenders are American, and one of them is the government's own pension fund.
2. **Three billion dollars a day.** Before America builds a road or pays a soldier, it spends about three billion dollars every day on interest alone.
3. **The year America owed nothing.** In January 1835 the national debt was zero. It lasted about a year, and it has never happened again.
4. **Three downgrades.** Since 2011 all three big rating agencies have taken away America's top credit score. It still borrows more easily than almost anyone. Why?
5. **More than the military.** America now pays more in interest on its debt than it spends on its entire armed forces.
