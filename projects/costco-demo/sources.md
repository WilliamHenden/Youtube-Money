## Caveats
- These figures come from trade press and earnings summaries of Costco's results. For a published video, check them once more against Costco's own annual report (Form 10-K) and press release on investor.costco.com.
- "Cheap fuel" in the closing line refers to Costco's member-only fuel stations, which are widely described as priced below local competitors. It isn't given a number in the video.
